# CXA-011 Tighter stops after a market shock (shadow comparator)
Item: CXA-011 (ch. 22) · Lineage: shock_stops_F1 to shock_stops_F6 · Market: AU · Status: INCONCLUSIVE

## Hypothesis
When the market enters a shock state, raising a hold's stop to the higher of the current stop and that session's close less one average daily range improves the net outcome of the hold. It is run forward on the holds the Cortex already follows, in both arms, and only as a comparison: no live stop changes.
