# VAL-120 Intraday stream: one look per cell
Item: VAL-120 (ch. 21) · Lineage: intraday_stream_<cell> (v1, then one per quarter) · Market: AU · Status: meter only

## Hypothesis
The intraday stream's watch alerts, taken one calendar cell at a time, give candidates whose later market-adjusted move is positive on average. Each cell is a separate test with its own frozen plan and one look; the V2 rule (one look per cell, the state following the recorded result) is unchanged. M23 puts the same look through the registry and adds a meter.

## Pre-registration
The plan of a cell is the canonical text of its logged spec, frozen through the registry before any figure is computed; its sha256 is the registry hash. No plan file is committed because the cell is created in production. Decision D-1723 in docs/M23_design.md; a quarterly cell reads as fresh data under the D-670 rule, not a second look at old data (D-1608).

## Data
Source: production tables: the intraday stream's recorded candidates and their sessions
Window: the cell's own quarter
Universe: candidates the stream recorded in that quarter
Known-time rules: a candidate counts once its outcome session has closed
Caveats:
- the meter is the smaller of the measured candidates and their sessions, each against 60
- the evaluator's floors are pinned to the meter by a test

## Split
One look per cell on the cell's own data; no split

## n
Not committed: counted in production by the nightly meter `intraday_stream_v1` (and later `intraday_stream_v1_<year>Q<n>`) against 60

## Statistic
Unchanged from V2: the one-sided 90 % lower bound of the mean excess; PASS when it is above zero, NULL when it is not, INCONCLUSIVE (and spent) when the bound is not defined. A registry fault never changes the evaluation.

## Result
No figure is committed. The meter row is the record of progress; the look is taken once through the registry on the night the floors are met.

## Verdict
No verdict in this file: meters only. Proved on fixtures: the look goes through the registry first and the ledger cell records it after; a registry look whose cell result was lost is completed from the stored figures; a ledger result with no registry look is entered with its own date (`tests/research/meters/intraday.test.ts`, `tests/intraday/evaluator.test.ts`, AT-63).

## Looks
Read from the registry by the lineage prefix `intraday_stream_`; cells are taken in production, so none are in the offline ledger. Refusals: none recorded here
