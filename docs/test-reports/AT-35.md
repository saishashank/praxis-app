## M18 clause (c): the pick side credits dividends (ARN-123, D-956)

Before D-956 the pick outcome was price only while the benchmark was total return. New settlements now credit confirmed cash and special dividends going ex after the fill session and on or before the exit session, and record `outcome_basis`; rows already settled stay `price_only` and are never rewritten (trigger).
