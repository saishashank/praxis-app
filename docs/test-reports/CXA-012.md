# CXA-012 Survivorship haircut and matched controls
Item: CXA-012 (ch. 22) · Lineage: none (a record, no look is taken) · Market: AU · Status: INCONCLUSIVE

## Hypothesis
A history that holds only the codes listed today overstates what an investor earned, because the codes that fell and were delisted are missing. The record fixes, before any report uses it, how that bias is stated: a haircut in points per year, the share of codes delisted each year times an assumed loss on a delisting, shown beside the unadjusted figure. Nothing trades and nothing is tested; no look is taken.

## Pre-registration
Key survivorship_loss_assumed_pct = 50, bounds 30 to 80, fixed (registered in M23 A1, no new key). Decisions D-1740 to D-1742 in docs/M23_design.md. Committed with this report; no registry row is written because no statistic is looked at.

## Data
Source: the history extract of the build machine (kept outside the repository) and the app's own tables
Window: 2000-01-03 to 2026-10-22
Universe: 2051 listed codes; 1856 have daily bars, 194 have no history on the vendor (listed today, not delisted codes)
Known-time rules: industry before a market's first own instrument_class snapshot is the map of today
Caveats:
- survivors only: the extract lists the codes that trade today, so no delisted code is in it and the AU delisted share per year cannot be measured
- the loss on a delisting is assumed (50 %), not measured, and labelled so
- static industry: every session before the market's first own instrument_class snapshot reads today's map
- matched controls: unmatched, because there are no delisted codes to match against; they apply only from the first own universe snapshot

## Split
Full window, no look: this is a record of how the bias is stated, not a test

## n
Not applicable: no setups, sessions or blocks are counted and no outcome is read

## Statistic
Haircut (points per year) = delisted share per year (percent) x assumed loss on a delisting (percent) / 100, exact decimal arithmetic. Cost profile: none · parcel: none. Matched control: the report says "unmatched" until the first own universe snapshot. Haircut with and without: every yearly return is shown beside its adjusted value.

## Result
Points per year taken off a survivorship-biased figure, by delisted share per year and assumed loss:

| Delisted share per year | loss 30 % | loss 50 % (default) | loss 80 % |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 % | 0.3 | 0.5 | 0.8 |
| 2 % | 0.6 | 1 | 1.6 |
| 3 % | 0.9 | 1.5 | 2.4 |
| 5 % | 1.5 | 2.5 | 4 |

Known answer kept: 3 % x 50 % = 1.5 points a year. With the default, every value that existed before this task is unchanged; the home market's live figures do not read the module.

## Verdict
INCONCLUSIVE: this is a record, not a test. The AU delisted share per year is missing; it can be measured only from the market's own universe snapshots, and until then reports show the caveat and the assumed haircut table, not a measured haircut.
