## Known limits

- **Picks still open at the horizon are left out (D-1498).** The ledger holds no mark at the horizon and none is invented, so a pick that outlives `committee_horizon_sessions` is never measured: the trial's sample is the picks that settle in time. A signal with no closed row is `unsettled`, one that closed later is `late`; the page shows both counts. Listed for the Owner at merge time.
- The Committee switch stays off until 10 nights qualify (AT-76) and the trial runs forward only; no trial has run in production.

Nothing in this file claims a result that has not happened.
