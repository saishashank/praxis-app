# AT-75 AU constants read from the profile (M19, AU part)

Reference: V3 spec ch. 22 (CXA-001, CXA-002) and ch. 14 (AT-75). Decisions D-1003, D-1007, D-1008, D-1016, D-1030 to D-1034 and D-1051 in [M19_design.md](../M19_design.md). The requirement text lives there; this file does not restate it. M19 proves the AU clause only: the IN and US clauses and the sector series are M20 and later (D-1001); they are open by design and not claimed here.

## Home environment (TST-115)

Local run on the Windows build machine (vitest, node environment), the golden ZZ worlds of AT-32 and AT-50, throwaway libSQL files, no network (SEC-110). The baselines (`tests/acceptance/v3/replay-hash.baseline.json`, `tests/market/au-constants.snapshot.json`, `worker/test/fixtures/au-slots.snapshot.json`) were recorded on the pre-refactor tree and are never rewritten in M19.
