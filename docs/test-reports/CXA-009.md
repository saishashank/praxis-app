# CXA-009 Industry-group momentum test
Item: CXA-009 (ch. 22) · Lineage: sector_ic · Market: AU · Status: PASS

## Hypothesis
Industry groups that did better over the half year that ended a month before a month starts do better than the other groups in that month, so the monthly rank correlation across groups is positive on average. A pass would only keep the sector tilt in shadow and put the result on file for the shadow path (CTX-053); nothing trades.

## Data
Source: daily bars and the listed-company industry file of the build machine history extract (kept outside the repository)
Window: 2000-01-04 to 2026-10-22
Universe: the top-turnover tier of the app tier rule on every session, in the industry groups of the extract's current map
Known-time rules: a signal ends 21 sessions before the month start and an outcome starts at the month start session close; the industry map is today's map for every date
Caveats:
- static industry: every code is in the group the extract's file gives today, for all dates
- survivors only: the extract holds codes that still trade, so groups look better than they were
- codes with no group, or the group Not Applic (funds and trusts), are left out
- prices are split-adjusted vendor closes; dividends are not added
- the backfilled series is built here and is not imported into production

## Split
Full window, one look: the effect size needs about 95 monthly blocks and the months after 2021 are far fewer, so one look takes the full window; the two parts are reported and decide nothing

## n
4862 group-months (the report's setups) · 6720 sessions · 311 monthly blocks · floor 95 monthly blocks, met (counted before any outcome was read)

## Statistic
Mean over months of the rank correlation, across the month's industry groups, between each group's return over the 126 sessions that end 21 sessions before the month start and its return over the 21 sessions after the month start, less the month's group mean. A month needs at least 4 groups of at least 3 members; the lower bound is the one-sided stationary block-bootstrap bound (expected block 3 months). 311 months qualified, 0 had no defined correlation; the mean group count was 15.63. The development figure covers the months that start before 2022-01-01 and the holdout figure the months from then; both are shown beside the full figure and decide nothing.
Cost profile: none (an index figure, nothing is traded) · parcel: none
Matched control: the other groups of the same month
Haircut: none; the survivors-only caveat stands

## Result
Unit: thousandths of a rank correlation (1000 = a perfect ranking)

| Window | Mean | 90 % lower bound | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| development | 48.41 | - | 254 |
| holdout | 19.26 | - | 57 |
| full | 43.07 | 16.52 | 311 |

The holdout-only figure (the 57 months from 2022) is lower than the development figure: 19.26 against 48.41 thousandths. It is reported for the reader and is not a gate; the plan fixed one look over the full window, and only the full window's bound decides. A weaker recent half is a reason for the Owner review to read the result as modest, not a change of the verdict.

## Verdict
PASS: the lower bound of the mean monthly rank correlation is above zero
