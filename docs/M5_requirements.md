## 5. Gaps, contradictions and open questions (numbered)

1. **Production email gating.** BLD-024 (`12` l.62) keeps "emails … disabled" in production until the build passes. TST-115 (`10` l.150) requires "AT-20 watchdog fallback (one forced run in M5 with the review check enabled)" in production. PLT-074 (`02` l.104) skips email checks "for markets in data-only mode". The spec does not say which key or step turns production email on for the forced run and back off afterwards. **[CHECK]**.
2. **ASX live polling is a prerequisite for AT-18 Part A.** DAT-121 (`06` l.67) defaults ASX off; O-23 (`00` l.55) accepts the risk of automated personal-use access; `13` l.147 says O-23 "supersedes DAT-121 default-off". D-061 (`docs/07_decision_log.md` l.67) keeps `asx_enabled` default off and requires `ASX_LIVE` and `ASX_USER_AGENT` before live polling. The Owner needs to switch these on before AT-18 Part A can pass. **Owner prerequisite.**
3. **AT-20a and AT-20b have no environment.** TST-115 (`10` l.150) lists AT-18 part A and part B, AT-19, AT-20, AT-21 and AT-05 but does not name AT-20a or AT-20b. **[CHECK]**: suggested staging (recorded fixtures), as for EML-016 and EML-023 tests.

The brief's content (overnight news, calendar, plan) has no template, no LLM role and no section number. **[CHECK]**.
5. **Non-trading day sections.** EML-010 (`04` l.17) says that on non-trading days "the trading sections are replaced by research/learning and weekend-preparation content", and AT-20 (`14` l.45) says "(trading day: all 10 sections)". The spec does not say which sections a non-trading review contains or whether sections 1–10 keep their numbers. **[CHECK]**.
6. **Ch. 16 P1 items with an M5 back-end.** UXN-113, UXN-157, UXN-160, UXN-161, UXN-163 and UXN-251 are accepted P1 (`16` l.7, l.81–82, l.89–90, l.148). Their screens are M7 (`12` l.54, `docs/M4_requirements.md` §6). `12` l.65 says only that "back-end parts … join M5/M6". Which parts are in M5 (for example the quiet-hours rule in the sender, the P1 regime notice, the KPI row) is not stated. **[CHECK]**.
7. **Section 7a "New picks" and the picks back-end.** UXN-044 (`16` l.254) adds section 7a and says it counts toward the 90 KB limit. The picks back-end (AGT-112, AGT-113, ARN-095) is "M5/M6" (`12` l.65). ch.15 keys `picks_per_profile`, `pick_success_min_n` and `picks_max_per_family` exist (`15` ch.15 §15.8). Decide whether 7a is part of the M5 review. **[CHECK]**.

NFR-006 (`10` l.8–22) has rows for Sentinel blind, quota, backup, invariant, degrade and 401/403, but not for an ASX trip or a source switch. **[CHECK]**: add the row or state that the trip goes in-app only.
9. **"Digest" is undefined.** AGT-115 (`07` l.68) sends "P1/P2 in-app and digest"; UX-110 (`09` l.107) has a "per-tier immediate vs digest toggle". No section or email defines a digest of alerts. The nearest is section 8 "Sentinel recap" (EML-012 s8). **[CHECK]**: is the digest section 8 only?

The M2 build has two plain daily cron entries (`docs/test-map.md` l.98). The spec does not say whether two daily entries are allowed on Hobby. **[VERIFY]**.
11. **Watchdog runs on a schedule that may not fire.** PLT-071 (`02` l.82) and PLT-014a (l.25) use YAML `schedule:` for the 20:40 and 21:50 watchdog, and PLT-071 notes "unconfirmed 2026 reports of `schedule` not firing reliably".
