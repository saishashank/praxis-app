# M17 design: intraday watch and pick alerts

Inputs: `docs/M17_requirements.md` (ARN-110…115, EML-038…042, AGT-139…144, DAT-238…240, UXN-301…311, VAL-117…119, NFR-041/042, SEC-114, PLT-077; AT-60…AT-65; Q1–Q16), spec ch. 19 V2. Decisions continue from M16 (D-377); this milestone uses D-381…D-396.

Roles: Opus orchestrates; Sonnet builds each work package; a Fable reviewer checks the packages marked **F** before they merge, and the integrated milestone at the end.

## Shape

- Two streams, never mixed (ARN-110). **Pick alerts**: the 09:55 re-check arms every surviving V1 pick (`pick` row: limit = `buy_max_micro`, stop = `stop_micro`) through one adapter `armableOrders(market, session)`; a pure `evaluateAlert(state, quote)` turns delayed quotes into append-only `pick_alert` transitions (armed, hit, cleared). **Intraday watch**: a signed route runs every 30 minutes, joins today's Sentinel items to delayed quotes, applies the deterministic rule (catalyst AND move AND volume AND fresh quote) and the night-pick gates, writes `intraday_candidate`, e-mails "Attention needed", and hands the codes (code only) to the 18:10 Cortex run as extra input.
- Everything is shadow. All families are in shadow (D-379/D-380), so alerts arm on V1 picks, the Cortex chance shows "Not enough history yet" on every pick, intraday rows are shown "Unrated" and "unvalidated", and `intraday_stream_state` stays `shadow`. The code paths for allowed or live-tested families are built and proven with fixture states only.
- Nothing in M17 places or changes an order, book, ledger or lesson (ARN-111, SEC-114). The only writers are `pick_alert`, `intraday_candidate`, quote-run records and e-mail rows. No LLM import in the new modules (AGT-139).
- Scheduling is Worker route slots (signed POST to Vercel), no Actions minutes: `pick-arm` 09:55, `quote-poll` every 5 min 10:00 to 16:00, `intraday-scan` every 30 min 10:30 to 15:30 (early close ends at early close, `src/lib/data/calendar.ts`), measurement inside the EOD batch.
- $0 (O-64). Delayed quotes from the already-accepted free source only (O-26, UXN-048). SEC-110: ZZ codes and synthetic prices only; the "recorded" quote sessions are generated by a fixture generator, never captured.

## Decisions

Q1–Q16 of the requirements are adopted at their recommended defaults. "Listed" means listed for the Owner in the V2 PR.

- **D-381** Free source only: the existing accepted delayed source (O-26), batched only if the contract test fits; no paid or newly licensed source. A `source_register` row is added for the quote endpoint. Shadow Cortex decisions are not armed. (listed)
- **D-382** Unknown cumulative volume fails the volume condition (fail-closed); System Health shows "volume unavailable".
- **D-383** The `pick_min_chance` 55 % floor is dormant: active only for allowed or live-tested families; V1 rules gate the rest; the list is whatever V1 yields up to 10. (listed)
- **D-384** One adapter `armableOrders(market, session)`: V1 picks now, allowed-family Cortex orders later.
- **D-385** Pick alerts default on for the Owner, off for other roles.
- **D-386** Hand-over adds codes only (no levels, no priority); an empty candidate set leaves the Cortex output byte-identical.
- **D-387** Route slots, `requiresListing: true`; staging enables them, production listing happens at PR approval. (listed)
- **D-388** Held pick mail leaves as one digest at the end of quiet hours; nothing here is P0.
- **D-389** `pick_alert_emails_per_day` = 12.
- **D-390** Slope hide reads the existing `cortex_slope_hide_ub` (1.0, D-257); no second key.
- **D-391** `intraday_stream_state` has one writer, the VAL-118 evaluator; the Owner may only set shadow; a test greps for others.
- **D-392** Outside hours or on a weekend the Intraday section shows the last trading day's candidates with outcome.
- **D-393** One colour map `src/lib/ui/familyBadge.ts` for tiles, Strategies and the concentration line; a test greps for a second table.
- **D-394** Reason code `filled` added to ARN-114 (in-app only, no e-mail). (listed)
- **D-395** Only an open or quote beyond `chase_cap_pct` clears `above_chase_cap`; between limit and cap the alert stays armed.
- **D-396** The monthly alert report renders "No data yet" states until data accrues; descriptive only.

## Risks

- No free batched delayed-quote endpoint, or no cumulative volume: scan codes fall to what the budget allows, volume fails closed (D-381/382), the stream stays small.
- Free-quota exhaustion hurts other jobs: narrowing at 80 %, stop at 95 %, scan stops before anything else (NFR-042).
- Duplicate or burst e-mail: key-claim per pick per session, own cap, 5-minute batching (WP4 review).
- Order-boundary leak: AT-60 byte-identity check and a grep test that no new module imports the arena writers.
- Hand-over changes the Cortex replay hash: covered by the empty-set identity test (WP6 review).
- The V1 list change to 10 with a dormant floor changes the visible list: flagged in the PR (D-383).
- Worker slots misfire on early-close days: calendar-driven end time tested in WP5.
- Stale-data honesty: every tile and e-mail carries quote time and "delayed"; no "real-time" wording (NFR-041).
