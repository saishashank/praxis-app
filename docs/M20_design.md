## Shape

- **Nothing IN or US reaches production (PRD-040, BLD-050).** Production `markets_enabled` stays `["AU"]` and the M19 proofs stay green on every CI run (AT-68). Every M20 reader, fetcher, table and page sits behind the three M19 gates (`assertMarketEnabled` before a write, the 0035 guard triggers, `resolveMarket` on every page); a new market-keyed table adds its own two guard triggers in the same migration (D-1022). The build proves IN and US in **development mode** only: unit tests with hand-made known-answer fixtures, the local recorded-day harness on recorded fixtures (D-970), and the build machine's history extract (never committed).
- **AU byte-identical (A-20).** The three M19 identity tests (`replayHash`, `auConstants`, `auSlots`) are green on every branch and at exit; the AT-32 baseline is **not** rewritten (D-1116 keeps `schemaSha` identical for the AU night). An IN or US value is read only where `profile.code` selects it; AU paths keep their M19 shape.
- **Shared first, tracks in parallel (ch. 12 M20 row, BLD-050).** Stream A (the M19 follow-ups, migration 0036, the extract tooling, profile completion, the money and Cortex rule engines) lands before any IN or US write path exists; then IN, US and the switch-on stream run in parallel with disjoint files (registry modules resolve by convention so no two streams edit one file, D-1127).
- **Offline by default, network by exception (D-1092).** Everything in `praxis-app` is proven in CI without a request leaving the runner (the `recorded-day` network assertion stays the last step). Two things touch the network: the extract scripts on the build machine (the "fill" runs, Appendix A) and the DAT-101 source-report workflow (`workflow_dispatch`, no database, artefact only; AT-M1). Each fetcher has a recorded fixture for its tests and a polite client (declared User-Agent, per-host rate, backoff; D-1093). EDGAR is never called without the Owner-store contact (D-1094).
- **Switch-on is code only (MKT-111, AT-71).** The checklist, the two-step writer, the rollback with the kill at the next open, the audit event, the quota projection and the Markets page controls are built and tested; the Owner performs the switch after the merge (§20.6, A-22). The build never sets `markets_enabled`, `market.enabled` or `PRAXIS_MARKETS_ENABLED` in production.
- **Research honesty (BLD-051, CXA-015).** The per-market calibration (CXA-002 j) runs the M15 programme on each market's development split only; it spends **no** holdout look (D-1114); every family starts in shadow per market (D-949); the US survivorship caveat is attached to every US report (CXA-012).
- **$0, Turso ≤ 20 %, no OFFSET, no staging, ASX off, no secrets.** No new paid provider (FRED needs the Owner's free key: the fetcher refuses without it, like EDGAR); every new statement is a key probe or a bounded, keyset-paged read with a `rows-ok:` comment; the IN/US `data_only` recorded days run in a separate CI job (`recorded-day-dev`, D-1119) so the production-shape job is untouched; `worker/src/asx/*` is not edited; the only environment names added are variables and Owner-store keys listed in `scripts/ci/env-names.mjs` (no value anywhere).

## Risks and spec findings

- **AT-69 "8-category map" vs DAT-245's 11 categories.** The definition wins; the fixture set covers all 11. Listed (D-1108).
- **`us_universe_max` / `in_universe_max` vs `mkt.<CODE>.universe_max`.** One key (D-1098). Listed.
- **Rollback "writes no new row" vs "closes books at the next open".** The kill is the one write the spec itself requires; `enabled` stays 1 until the kill has run, so the guard does not refuse it, and is 0 immediately after (D-1101). The AT-71 test asserts no other table gains a row. **F**.
- **Latency measured on 10 nights cannot be done offline.** D-1105 defers the measurement to production `data_only` and makes `full` wait for 10 samples: additive to the 5-day rule. Listed.
- **The NSE 2027 holiday list is not published until December 2026.** Draft rows, re-drafted by OPS-103 (D-1097); the calendar `confirmed` flag already models it.
- **Network reachability of the IN sources.** NSE and BSE block bots; the fill may fail for the archive or the daily files. Then the IN profile stays incomplete by name, the register rows stay `pending`/`disabled`, and A-18 keeps IN off until a price source passes — honest and recorded, not a stub.
- **EDGAR without the contact.** The US extract loses the filings history (the announcement angles are forward-only per market anyway, §20.4) and the company-facts history (F2 "no-fundamentals" variant, CXA-013); AT-38 and the 8-K map are proven on hand-made fixtures. Listed (D-1094).
- **Verification may change a constant.** Every IN-P1/US-P1 rate and the IN tick rule is confirmed by A3 against its public page; a change re-derives the fixtures in the same commit and is a decision line. The seed keeps `verify = 1` with the evidence reference either way (DAT-242).
- **Turso.** New tables are written only in development (no production row); the guard probe cost is unchanged; the projection (D-1099) is the first quantitative statement of IN/US cost and must show AU + IN + US ≤ 20 % — if it does not at the caps, the caps are the Owner's lever (MKT-113), recorded in the PR.
- **Five Sonnet builders on the shared phase.** A2 is on the critical path (migration, `scriptDb`, zones); A1 and A3 need the network and hours of wall time — start A1's fill runs before A2 so the extract is there when A4 needs `buckets.json`.
- **`holdout_look` table before its UI (M23).** Created in 0036 so the registry exists when the first per-market look is ever spent; M20 writes no row (D-1114).
- **Recorded-day job and the development shape.** The variable is allowed only in `recorded-day-dev` (D-1119); `e2e-listing.test.mjs` is the guard against a workflow edit leaking it into a lane.
- **No Owner prompt.** Every open point is decided above and in Appendix C; the Owner's actions are after the merge (§20.6) and listed in exit §9.
